How to play

Every day, each topic has a new puzzle at two difficulties, each with a secret solution. Work out what it is by asking up to 21 yes-or-no questions. Everyone gets the same puzzle, so you can compare with friends.

Answers

  • Yes clearly true
  • No clearly false
  • Partially true for part of it, or only to a degree (try splitting your question)
  • Depends can't be answered as asked (try being more specific)
  • Correct! you got it

After a game, tap Why? next to any answer to see the reasoning.

Difficulty

Normal solutions are things most people would recognize. Expert solutions need real knowledge of the topic.

Hints

A hint unlocks every 4 questions.

Sharing

After a game, Share result sends a spoiler-free summary, one emoji per question, with a link to your game. Friends who haven't played that puzzle yet are asked to play it first.

Topics

Suggest your own topic at the bottom of Today. Puzzles for it take a minute or two to create, and very narrow topics may be turned down.

Tips

  • Start broad (era, place, field) before narrowing down.
  • Partially and Depends tell you how to rephrase.
  • Ask in any language. Answers come back in English.
